# Use it for

## Marketing

Know about competitor launches the week they happen and decide fast whether to respond with content or positioning.

## GTM

Walk into a deal knowing what a competitor changed on pricing, packaging, or product this week.

## Product

Track what competitors ship without a manual review, with a source link for every claim.
